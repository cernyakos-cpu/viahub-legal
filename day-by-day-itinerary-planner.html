How the day-by-day plan works

After the AI trip planner suggests things to do, ViaHub lays out your trip day by day. Activities you selected are placed automatically; the ones you skipped wait in a planning pool, ready if you change your mind.

Drag-and-drop activity tiles

Every activity is a compact tile. Drag it to another day to rearrange your itinerary in seconds.

Rest days and beach days

Add a rest day whenever you need a break. Beach days are available on days in coastal cities.

Light travel days

In multi-city trips, activities are kept off arrival and departure days, so you're not rushing after a flight.

Your own activities

Type in anything you want to do. If it names a city on your route, it can only go on that city's days.

Multi-city itineraries made simple

Each city on your route gets its own colour, matched on the route, the day boxes and the activity tiles. Change the city order with drag and drop, adjust the nights in each city within your trip length, and the itinerary rearranges itself. See it in practice in our Oslo & Copenhagen itinerary.

From draft itinerary to booked trip

When your bookings are made, convert the plan into a real trip. Each activity lands on your travel timeline as a card you can open to add times and details.

Frequently asked questions

Can I move activities between days?

Yes. Activities are tiles you can drag onto any day of the trip.

Can I add a rest day to my itinerary?

Yes. Rest days can go on any day; beach days are offered on days in coastal cities.

Does it work for trips to several cities?

Yes. Each city has its own colour, and you can reorder cities and change the nights in each.
