One trip, everyone on the same page

Group trips fall apart in chat threads: one person has the flight details, another the hotel, and nobody knows the plan for Saturday. In ViaHub you invite your companions to the trip, and everyone sees the same itinerary, stays and timeline.

  • Invite companions by name and email address and link them to the trip.
  • Share the plan: flights, accommodation and excursions on one travel timeline.

Why use a trip app instead of a spreadsheet?

A shared spreadsheet works until someone forgets to add the taxi from the airport. Keeping costs inside the trip means every expense sits next to the booking it belongs to, the totals update by themselves and nobody has to be the group's accountant.

Frequently asked questions

How do I invite travel companions?

Open the trip and invite them with their name and email address. They are linked to the trip, so you can share the plan and split costs.

Can ViaHub split costs unevenly?

ViaHub splits shared costs between the travellers on a trip. In AI cost estimates, children under 6 are not counted as paying travellers.

Is my trip data visible to my companions?

Companions see the shared trip. Your logged GPS route is private and never visible to companions.
