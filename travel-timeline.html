Frequently asked questions

What appears on the ViaHub timeline?

Flights, accommodation, booked excursions and AI-suggested activities, in date order from the first leg to the way home.

What does a red gap on the timeline mean?

It shows that the city changes between two consecutive cards without a journey in between, so you know a leg is missing.
