Frequently asked questions

Can I plan a road trip with a rental car?

Yes. A rental car is always available as a local transport and between-cities option, and its cost is added to your estimate.

Does changing the transport option change the trip cost?

Yes. The cost estimate updates when you switch between public transport, taxi and a rental car.
