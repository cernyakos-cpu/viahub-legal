The three things people forget before a trip

  1. Checking passport validity. Many countries want your passport to be valid for months after you leave. A reminder before it expires saves a lot of stress.
  2. Entry permits and visas. Even short trips can need an electronic travel authorisation. Check the requirements for your nationality well before departure.
  3. Travel insurance. Easy to skip, expensive to miss. Put it on the list the moment you book.
Entry rules change. Always confirm requirements with official government sources before you travel.

Frequently asked questions

Does ViaHub tell me if I need a visa?

ViaHub shows visa, insurance and vaccination information for your nationality and destination, with links to useful sources. Always confirm with official sources before you travel.

Does ViaHub store my passport?

No. You can optionally save the expiry date of your ID card, passport or driving licence for reminders. ViaHub never stores document numbers, scans or photos.

Is there a packing list?

Yes. Every trip checklist includes a packing list you can tick off as you pack, helped by the weather outlook for each stop.
