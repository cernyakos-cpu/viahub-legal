AI Trip Planner

An AI trip planner that drafts your whole trip

Tell ViaHub where and when you want to go. In a few minutes you get ideas for things to do, a cost estimate for your whole group and a day-by-day plan you can drag and edit.

How the AI trip planner works

  1. Tell it where and when. Pick one or more destinations and your dates. Flexible dates are fine, too.
  2. Set what matters to you. Number of travellers (including children under 6), food style and how you like to get around: public transport, taxi or a rental car.
  3. Pick things to do. The planner suggests activities for every city. Choose the ones you like; the rest stay in a planning pool for later.
  4. Get your plan. A cost estimate, a day-by-day draft, a weather outlook and the travel requirements for your trip, all in one place.

What's in an AI trip plan

Day-by-day itinerary

Activities shown as tiles you can drag onto any day. Add rest days, and beach days in coastal cities.

Cost estimate

Flights, stays, food and local transport for the whole group, with options sorted from cheapest to most expensive.

Weather outlook

An AI outlook for every stop, so you know what to pack before you go.

Travel requirements

Visa, insurance and vaccination information for your nationality, with useful links.

Built for multi-city trips

Planning Oslo and Copenhagen in one go, or a week across three Italian cities? ViaHub chooses a sensible route order for you, and you can change it with drag and drop. Each city gets its own colour on the route, the days and the activities, so you always see where you are.

  • Change the order of cities and the number of nights in each, within your trip length.
  • Activities tied to a city can only go on that city's days, so nothing ends up in the wrong place.
  • Arrival and departure days are kept light, because nobody wants a museum marathon after a flight.

See an example in our 6-day Oslo & Copenhagen itinerary.

From plan to a real trip

When you've booked, tap "I have my exact travel details" and the plan becomes a real trip. The AI's cost findings move to your trip finances as planned costs, booking links go to your travel checklist, and every activity lands on your trip timeline, ready for times and details.

Planning with friends? Invite them to the trip and split the costs.

No waiting around

Big trips can take a moment to plan. If it takes longer than about 30 seconds, ViaHub keeps working in the background: your plan appears as "Planning" on your departures board, and a notification tells you when it's ready. Tap it and you're straight back in your plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is the ViaHub AI trip planner free?

ViaHub is free to download on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Can I plan a trip with flexible dates?

Yes. Tell the planner roughly when you want to travel and it builds a plan around that.

Can the AI plan a trip to several cities?

Yes. Add multiple cities and ViaHub suggests a route order and nights per city. You can reorder cities by drag and drop and change the nights in each.

Can I edit the AI's plan?

Yes. Activities are tiles you can drag between days, and you can add rest days, beach days in coastal cities, or your own activities.

Which devices does ViaHub work on?

ViaHub runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 26.5 or later, and on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
