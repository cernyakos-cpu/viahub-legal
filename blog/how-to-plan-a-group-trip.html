How to Plan a Group Trip Without the Chaos

Group trips fall apart in the group chat: lost messages, unanswered polls, and nobody sure who booked what. Here is how to plan a group trip with friends or family so everyone stays informed and the money stays fair.

By the ViaHub team · 30 October 2026 · 7 min read

Appoint one organiser, share the decisions

Group trips need exactly one person who keeps the plan together, even if decisions are shared. The organiser creates the trip, collects confirmations and makes sure everyone can see the same information. Without that role, everybody assumes someone else booked the apartment.

Agree three things before anyone books

  • Dates. Pick a date range everyone can actually do, and confirm it before buying anything non-refundable.
  • Budget range. Talk about money early. A trip that is cheap for one person can be a stretch for another. Agree a per-person ceiling for accommodation and a rough daily budget.
  • Travel style. Busy sightseeing or slow beach days? One honest conversation here prevents a week of quiet frustration.

Keep one source of truth, not five group chats

Chats are great for banter and terrible for information. Flight times scroll away, hotel addresses get buried and screenshots multiply. Put the actual plan in one place that everyone can open: flights, accommodation, activities and times.

With ViaHub you add your travel companions to the trip. They register (or log in) and instantly see the whole trip, always up to date. If your friend does not have the app yet, you can invite them by email. See group trips and expense splitting.

Split costs as you go, not at the end

The most common reason friendships get awkward on holiday is money. Track shared costs the moment they happen and settle once at the end. Decide who pays for what, then split by even share or by who took part. Our full guide on splitting travel expenses with friends explains the method step by step.

Plan around different people, not the average

In any group, people want different things. Build the itinerary with a few fixed group activities, then leave optional blocks where people can split up. ViaHub lets individual travellers opt out of a booked activity so the cost split stays accurate.

Give everyone the right level of access

Not everyone needs to edit the whole plan. Let the organiser and one trusted co-planner edit, and let everyone else view. That keeps the itinerary tidy while still letting each person see their own flights and rooms.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app for planning a group trip?

Look for an app that lets everyone see the same up-to-date plan and tracks shared costs. ViaHub does both: invite travel companions to a trip and split expenses inside the app.

How do I plan a trip with friends who live in different cities?

Agree dates and a budget first, then let each person book their own transport to the meeting point. Use a shared trip plan so everyone sees arrival times and the accommodation address.

Can I invite someone who does not have the app yet?

Yes. In ViaHub you can type any email address. If the person is not on ViaHub yet, they receive an invitation and see the whole trip after they register with that email.
