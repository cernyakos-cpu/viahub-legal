How to Keep All Your Travel Bookings in One Place

Flights in one email, hotel in another, tour tickets in a PDF, restaurant in a screenshot. Here is how to gather every booking into a single clear timeline you can open offline at the airport.

By the ViaHub team · 30 October 2026 · 5 min read

The problem with scattered confirmations

When bookings live across emails, chats and screenshots, you lose time exactly when you can least afford it: at check-in, at a border or at a hotel desk at midnight. A single organised trip view removes that stress.

What to store for every booking

  • Flights and trains: times, terminals or platforms, booking reference.
  • Accommodation: address, check-in and check-out times, reservation number, contact details.
  • Activities and tours: meeting point, time, ticket or voucher.
  • Transport extras: airport transfers, car rental pick-up details.

Use one timeline per trip

A timeline shows your trip in the order it happens: flight out, hotel, tour, train, flight home. In ViaHub the travel timeline holds every leg of a trip, and your departures board on the home screen shows what is coming up next, like an airport display.

Share only what companions need

Travel companions can see the shared trip, so nobody needs to ask you for the hotel address. Costs you split are visible only to the people they involve.

Prepare for the no-signal moment

Whatever app you use, keep the most important information (reservation numbers and addresses) easy to open, and consider screenshots of tickets as a backup. Our travel planning checklist lists what to prepare before you leave.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best way to organise travel bookings?

Put every booking on a single trip timeline in a trip organizer app, with reference numbers and addresses, and share it with the people travelling with you.

Can I share my itinerary with travel companions?

Yes. In ViaHub you add travel companions to a trip. They register and instantly see all the up-to-date trip information.

How do I find my booking numbers quickly?

Store each reservation number next to the matching leg or hotel in your trip, at the time you book.
