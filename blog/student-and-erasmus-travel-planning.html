Student and Erasmus Travel Planning: Great Trips on a Small Budget

Studying abroad means a new city every few weeks and a group chat full of plans. Here is how to plan student and Erasmus trips that are cheap, organised and easy to share.

By the ViaHub team · 30 October 2026 · 6 min read

Plan around your timetable

Block out exam periods and deadlines first, then look for long weekends and breaks. Booking early for the same cheap weekend everyone else wants usually saves the most. Check public holidays in both your host country and your destination.

Keep the budget visible

Students tend to budget by weekend rather than by trip. Estimate each weekend with the five-bucket method: transport, accommodation, food, local transport and activities, plus a small buffer. Choose hostels or apartments with a kitchen to cut costs.

Coordinate friends without chaos

Student groups change constantly: someone cancels, someone joins last minute. Use one shared trip plan rather than scattering details across chats. With ViaHub you invite travel companions by email: they sign up and see every detail instantly. See how to plan a group trip.

Split costs from day one

Track shared costs like the apartment, the train tickets and group dinners as they happen, and settle at the end. It keeps friendships intact. Details in how to split travel expenses with friends.

Make a travel history you will love later

Every trip becomes part of your story. ViaHub's travel statistics show countries visited and distance travelled, and the route tracker records your path during a trip. Nice to look back on after the semester ends.

Safety and documents

Keep digital and paper copies of your passport or ID, insurance and student documents. Share your itinerary with a trusted person at home. Check entry rules if you hold a non-EU passport.
