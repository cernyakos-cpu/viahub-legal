Travel Planning Checklist: 30 Things to Sort Before You Fly

Use this travel checklist to cover the essentials: documents, money, bookings, health, packing and home. Work through it in the weeks before departure and you will arrive relaxed.

By the ViaHub team · 30 October 2026 · 6 min read

Four to six weeks before

  • Check that your passport is valid long enough (many countries require several months beyond your return date).
  • Check visa or electronic travel authorisation requirements for your nationality.
  • Book flights and accommodation, and save every confirmation in one place.
  • Arrange travel insurance.
  • Check vaccination or health recommendations for your destination.
  • Set a budget and estimate daily costs.

Two weeks before

  • Check the weather forecast for each stop.
  • Plan airport transfers and local transport.
  • Book tickets for must-see attractions and restaurants.
  • Tell your bank you are travelling, and check card fees abroad.
  • Share the trip with travel companions.
  • Download offline maps and translation packs.

The week of departure

  • Check in online if available (usually 24 hours before a flight).
  • Pack, using the weather outlook as a guide.
  • Charge power banks and pack adapters.
  • Print or save tickets and key addresses.
  • Set an out-of-office or arrange for post and plants.

What to carry in your hand luggage

  • Passport or ID, boarding passes, insurance details.
  • Phone, charger and a small power bank.
  • Medication, a change of clothes and essential toiletries.
  • A little local currency or a travel card.
Entry rules and airline policies change. Always double-check requirements with official sources close to departure.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do before travelling abroad?

Check passport validity and entry requirements, arrange insurance, book and save confirmations, check the weather, tell your bank, share the plan and pack.

How far ahead should I check visa requirements?

At least four to six weeks before departure, because some visas and authorisations take days or weeks to process.
