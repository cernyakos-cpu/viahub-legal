What ViaHub tracks

Distance travelled

The kilometres you've covered across your trips.

Countries visited

A growing list of the countries you've been to.

Journey to the Moon

Your total distance compared with the trip to the Moon. How far along are you?

Daily distance

With route logging on, see how far you travelled each day of a trip.

Powered by your routes

Statistics build on your trips and on the optional route tracker, which logs your real route only during an active trip and pauses when you stand still. Your location history is private and only visible to you.

Frequently asked questions

How does ViaHub count distance travelled?

Distance comes from your trips and, if you turn it on, the route logged during active trips.

Can I track the countries I've visited?

Yes. Your travel statistics include the countries you've visited.
