ViaHub vs Wanderlog

Both apps help you plan a trip. Wanderlog is built around a map you fill with places; ViaHub drafts the plan for you with AI and includes costs from the start.

Choose Wanderlog if…

  • You enjoy researching every place yourself and pinning it on a map.
  • You plan on a laptop as much as on your phone, or your group uses Android.
  • Route optimisation and offline maps are must-haves (Pro).

Choose ViaHub if…

  • You'd rather get a ready-made draft from AI and adjust it than start from a blank map.
  • You want to know what the trip will cost before you book.
  • You want visa, insurance and packing in a travel checklist next to your plan.
  • You're on iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Frequently asked questions

Is ViaHub a Wanderlog alternative?

Yes. Both are trip planners. ViaHub drafts the itinerary and cost estimate with AI; Wanderlog is centred on adding places to a shared map.

Which is better for group trips?

Both support group trips. Wanderlog offers live collaborative editing; ViaHub combines a shared trip with automatic cost splitting.
