TripIt organises the trips you've already booked. ViaHub helps you plan the trip in the first place, then keeps it organised. Here's how they compare.

Choose TripIt if…

  • You travel often for work and your trips are booked by the time you open the app.
  • Real-time flight alerts, gate reminders and airport navigation matter most to you.
  • You or your companions use Android or want a web version.

Choose ViaHub if…

  • You want help deciding what to do and how much the trip will cost before you book.
  • You plan multi-city trips and want a day-by-day draft you can drag around.
  • You travel with friends or family and want to split costs in the same app.
  • You like a checklist for visas, documents and packing, and a log of the route you travelled.

Frequently asked questions

Is ViaHub a TripIt alternative?

Yes, for leisure travellers who want planning help. ViaHub plans the trip with AI and keeps bookings, costs and companions together; TripIt focuses on organising existing bookings and flight alerts.

Is ViaHub available on Android?

No. ViaHub is available for iPhone, iPad and Mac.
