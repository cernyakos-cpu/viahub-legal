Cost Estimate

Know what your trip will cost before you book

ViaHub estimates flights, stays, food and transport for your whole group, so you can set a realistic travel budget and compare options side by side.

What the trip cost estimate includes

Flights

Flight options sorted from cheapest to most expensive, with the price highlighted.

Accommodation

Places to stay in every city on your route, also sorted by price.

Food

Pick your food style and watch the daily food budget change with it.

Transport

Public transport, taxi or a rental car in each city, plus options for getting between cities.

A travel budget that fits your group

  • Set the number of travellers, and costs are calculated for the whole group.
  • Children under 6 aren't counted as paying travellers when costs are split.
  • Every option list has a default from your profile preferences, so you only change what's different this time.

From estimate to real travel expenses

When the trip becomes real, the estimate moves to your trip finances as planned costs. Pick the options you booked, overwrite amounts with real prices and split the costs with your companions.

Estimates are a planning guide. Real prices depend on dates, availability and the provider you book with.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the trip cost estimate?

It's a planning guide based on typical options for your destination and dates. Real prices depend on availability and where you book.

Can I change the transport or food budget?

Yes. Choosing a different food style or local transport option updates the estimate.

Is ViaHub free?
